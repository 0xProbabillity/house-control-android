## Full description (4000)
House Control shows your home as a live 3D model on your phone or tablet. Lamps light the rooms the way they light your real ones, an air conditioner that runs blows visible air, and one tap on the model switches the real device. No account, no ads, no analytics.

YOUR FLOOR PLAN IN 3D
• Rooms, walls, doors, windows and furniture, drawn from one plan. Homes on several floors, one floor at a time.
• Turn the home all the way round with one finger; two fingers move it and pinch to zoom, so every lamp is within reach.

LIGHTS, AIR CONDITIONERS AND SCENES
• Tap a lamp in the 3D view or its tile to toggle it, drag across a tile to dim, touch and hold for brightness, white temperature and colour.
• Air conditioners: power, target temperature, mode, fan speed, swing, sleep and eco, whatever the unit offers, with the room temperature from a real sensor if you have one.
• Scenes set every light and AC at once: Cozy, Movie, Night, or your own. Tick "On/off button" and a scene becomes a room button: one tap on, the next tap off.

A FLOOR PLAN EDITOR MADE FOR A FINGER
• Draw rooms, drop in furniture, place lamps and ACs, pinch to zoom, and link each one to a real device, all on the phone.
• Before a file or a paired computer replaces your home, the one there is kept: Settings → Earlier homes brings it back.

YOUR DEVICES
• Home Assistant (and the thousands of brands it runs, including Matter and HomeKit accessories you add to it): found on your Wi-Fi by itself, live updates.
• Philips Hue: the Hue Bridge on your Wi-Fi, found by itself; press its button once.
• Until you connect something, every device is simulated, so you can try everything first with the demo homes.

WIDGETS, SHORTCUTS AND A TILE
• Your home from above with its lamps glowing, your scenes and your lights on the home screen; scenes in the app icon's menu; "All lights" in Quick Settings. Every tap switches the real devices without opening the app.

OPTIONAL: YOUR COMPUTER
• Pair with House Control on a Mac or a Windows PC by scanning its code, and the floor plan, device links and connections stay the same on both, over your own Wi-Fi with an encrypted link.

PRIVACY
House Control has no account and no servers of its own. Your plan and connections stay on the phone; tokens and keys are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and left out of backups. The app talks only to your own Home Assistant, your Hue Bridge and your paired computer.
